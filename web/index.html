    ANALYSIS METHOD

    Evidence before interpretation.

    YOLOv8s detects generic aircraft using COCO weights. Overlapping tiles can help with small assets; duplicate boxes are suppressed by overlap.

    Scene assessment

    Activity measures detected asset presence: 0–2 LOW, 3–6 MEDIUM, 7+ HIGH. Aircraft groups are connected by a configurable proximity distance relative to image diagonal. Isolated aircraft have no nearby aircraft.

    Change assessment

    Trend score = 2 × aircraft change + vehicle change + 2 × activity rank change + 2 × proximity density change. Scores ≥2 indicate Increased, ≤−2 Decreased; otherwise Stable. Density is the share of possible aircraft pairs that are nearby.

    Review priority is High for an absolute change of ≥2 aircraft or ≥4 vehicles; Medium for one aircraft, any vehicles, or a density shift of ≥25 percentage points; otherwise Low. Increases and decreases both receive review priority.

    Interpretation limits

    Images must cover the same ground area and scale. The system does not align images, identify aircraft types, or track individual assets. Counts and confidence scores do not establish movement, military intent, or threat. Verify the annotated boxes and imagery manually.

    Uploads and outputs are saved locally in the project’s outputs/runs folder. First analysis downloads the public model weights if needed.
